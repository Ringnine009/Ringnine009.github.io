01 Problem

Production LLM apps are easy to demo but hard to understand: the model itself is a black box. A from-scratch implementation makes the full stack — tokenization, attention, training dynamics — legible.

Offline, zero-API-cost chat is a real need (e.g. the offline layer of MycoGuard).

02 Approach

Implement a decoder-only GPT-style transformer from scratch in PyTorch — byte-pair tokenizer, data pipeline, transformer blocks, training loop and sampling.

Pre-train on a small corpus, then QLoRA fine-tune Qwen2.5-1.5B on the 8 GB laptop GPU; serve streaming inference over FastAPI with a chat UI.
