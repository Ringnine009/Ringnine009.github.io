MycoGuard

Mushroom risk assessment that never over-claims: an offline rule engine with live SVG rendering, Qwen-VL photo identification, and explicit uncertainty tiers.

Feature walkthrough

  • Detailed SVG mushroom rendering — gradients, textures, animated transitions
  • Offline rule engine + real-time morphology rendering
  • Photo identification with vision-sourced trait badges
  • Graded uncertainty tiers, never absolute claims
  • Built-in safety-knowledge Q&A with sources

Measured results

  • UCI Mushrooms dataset, all 8,124 rows: 0 of 3,916 poisonous samples graded low risk — the failure mode that matters — at a cost of 8.79% false alarms (370 of 4,208 edible).
  • A single-trait “odor” lookup scores higher raw accuracy (98.52%) than the full engine, but mis-grades 3.06% of poisonous samples as low risk — hence the headline metric is false-safe count, not accuracy.
  • The displayed number is evidence strength (how much was observed), not a probability of safety: no verdict out-displays a higher-risk tier, and the |Δ| formula was rebuilt after an audit.
  • The photo demo is the real model, not a scripted one: the recording drives the live qwen-vl-plus endpoint with a real field photograph, and the vision notes it shows were written by the model.
  • The demo also forced a language fix: the prompt demanded Chinese notes unconditionally, so an English reader saw a Chinese description. The UI language now reaches the prompt.
